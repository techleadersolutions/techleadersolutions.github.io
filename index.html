Meet John

Business leadership.
Technology depth.

Better outcomes begin with how we lead.

I spent nearly three decades at Vanguard, including almost ten years as Global Chief Information Officer. I also led two distinct businesses with P&L responsibility: first High Net Worth and later Wealth Management. I led approximately 6,000 technology professionals and a roughly $2 billion annual technology budget. That combination shaped my belief that business and technology strategy belong in the same conversation.

As U.S. CIO in Residence at Deloitte, I contribute in three ways: advising the firm on strategy and go-to-market priorities; working with Deloitte clients and their executive teams on technology strategy, AI adoption and governance, operating model transformation, cybersecurity, and technology risk; and developing current and future technology leaders as lead keynote speaker and content advisor for Deloitte’s Next Generation CIO Academies. I also chair Deloitte’s CIO Advisory Council.

Through Technology Leadership Solutions, the firm I founded and lead, I advise clients on business and technology strategy, organizational transformation, and leadership effectiveness. My work includes executive coaching and development, startup consulting, and advising founders and venture investors. I serve as Strategic Advisor to Myriad Venture Partners, Advisor to Fleet, and Senior Advisor to AIIRIFY.

As a keynote speaker and thought leader, I explore how AI is changing work, decisions, and the responsibilities of leaders and boards. My perspectives have appeared in The Wall Street Journal, Harvard Business Review, Business Insider, and Forbes. My approach is practical: connect strategy, people, technology, and governance, empower teams, and continually rethink how work gets done to deliver better customer outcomes and lasting business value.

Boards, startups & venture advisory

Business judgment.
Technology depth.

Technology investment, cybersecurity, and AI governance are enterprise decisions. I bring experience leading technology and businesses in highly regulated financial services, with direct P&L responsibility and nearly a decade reporting to Vanguard’s Chairman and CEO.

My board-facing experience includes cybersecurity oversight, enterprise resilience, modernization, and large-scale capital investment. I previously served as Board Chair and Nominating Committee Lead for Cradles to Crayons Philadelphia.

I also advise startups and venture firms: AIIRIFY as Senior Advisor, Fleet as Advisor, and Myriad Venture Partners as Strategic Advisor. These roles bring a practical perspective on emerging technology, enterprise adoption, and growth.
