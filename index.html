Meet John

A technology leader.
A business perspective.

Better outcomes begin with how we lead.

I spent nearly 30 years at Vanguard, including almost a decade as Global CIO. My experience spans technology, global operations, wealth management, and building a scalable advice business. That mix shaped my belief that technology strategy and business strategy belong in the same conversation. I led a global organization of approximately 6,000 technology professionals and a roughly $2 billion annual technology budget.

Today, I serve as U.S. CIO in Residence at Deloitte and chair its CIO Advisory Council. Through Technology Leadership Solutions, I work with executives and organizations on the leadership and operating changes that make transformation possible.

My approach is practical: clarify the outcome, empower the people closest to the work, and build an organization that keeps learning.

Boards, startups & venture advisory

Business judgment.
Technology depth.

Technology investment, cybersecurity, and AI governance are enterprise decisions. I bring experience leading technology and businesses in highly regulated financial services, with direct P&L responsibility and nearly a decade reporting to Vanguard’s Chairman and CEO.

My board-facing experience includes cybersecurity oversight, enterprise resilience, modernization, and large-scale capital investment. I previously served as Board Chair and Nominating Committee Lead for Cradles to Crayons Philadelphia.

I also advise startups and venture firms: AIIRIFY as Senior Advisor, Fleet as Advisor, and Myriad Venture Partners as Strategic Advisor. These roles bring a practical perspective on emerging technology, enterprise adoption, and growth.
