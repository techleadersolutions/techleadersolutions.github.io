Executive Profile

John T. Marcante

U.S. CIO in Residence at Deloitte and former Global CIO of Vanguard.

John T. Marcante is a business and technology leader, executive advisor, board candidate and keynote speaker who helps executives and boards turn technology and AI into business value. He is Founder and Managing Partner of Technology Leadership Solutions and Chair of Deloitte's CIO Advisory Council.

His work focuses on technology strategy, artificial intelligence and AI governance, cybersecurity, enterprise transformation, operating models, financial services, executive leadership and board advisory.

Leadership and advisory work

At Deloitte, John serves as U.S. CIO in Residence, working with technology executives and leadership teams on strategy, transformation and the evolving role of the CIO. Through Technology Leadership Solutions, he provides strategic advisory, executive coaching, speaking and board perspectives.

Previously, John served as Global CIO and Managing Director of Vanguard, where his leadership responsibilities spanned technology, cybersecurity, risk, transformation and global operations.
