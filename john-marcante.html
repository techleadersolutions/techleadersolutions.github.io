Executive Profile

John T. Marcante

U.S. CIO in Residence at Deloitte and former Global CIO of Vanguard.

John T. Marcante is a technology and financial services executive who has led both a global technology organization and client-facing wealth businesses. He specializes in AI governance and business value, helping executives and boards decide where to invest, establish oversight and accountability, and measure the results AI delivers.

Vanguard leadership

John spent nearly three decades at Vanguard, including almost ten years as Global CIO and Managing Director. He reported directly to two successive Vanguard chairmen and CEOs. As a member of the senior leadership team, he oversaw the technology Vanguard uses to serve clients and manage investments. He led a broad technology modernization that included cloud adoption and changes to how teams worked and delivered software.

Before becoming Global CIO, John ran Vanguard’s High Net Worth and Wealth Management businesses and held leadership roles in institutional technology and global technology operations. His experience running client businesses informs how he evaluates technology decisions, from their impact on clients to how leaders measure results. He received the 2020 Philadelphia CIO of the Year ORBIE Award and the 2016 Forbes CIO Innovation Award.

Deloitte strategy and client advisory

As Deloitte’s U.S. CIO in Residence, John advises Deloitte leaders on business strategy and go-to-market priorities. He works directly with clients on technology strategy, enterprise transformation and executive leadership. He chairs Deloitte’s CIO Advisory Council, serves as an associate dean and speaker at the Next Generation CIO Academies, and mentors executives through Deloitte’s CIO Program and CIO Transition Labs.

Board and venture advisory

John founded Technology Leadership Solutions to advise executives and boards on technology strategy, AI governance and cybersecurity oversight. His AI work connects investment decisions with governance and measurable outcomes. He helps leadership teams identify where AI can improve how work gets done, define what success looks like, and set the oversight and accountability needed as AI systems take on more autonomous work. He serves on the Executive Advisory Board of Myriad Venture Partners and advises Fleet and AIIRIFY. He previously served as board co-chair and led the nominating committee at Cradles to Crayons Philadelphia.

Writing and speaking

John contributes to The Wall Street Journal’s CIO Journal and CIO TechWorld, publishes original articles on LinkedIn, and coauthors The Adaptive Enterprise, published on Barry O’Reilly’s site. His writing and keynotes explore what boards should ask about technology and AI, how leaders can govern systems that act with greater autonomy, and how to measure whether AI changes how work gets done and improves business outcomes.
